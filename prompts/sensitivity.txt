You are an expert conversation analyst. You will be given a full conversation between User A (human) and User B (chatbot). Code only User A messages for sensitive material. 

# Definition 

You are looking for sensitive material: things people are generally reluctant to raise with others because they carry stigma, shame, exposure, or censure.

Most messages are not sensitive. Ordinary requests, ordinary questions, ordinary personal facts are not sensitive. Do not look for a reason to say YES. When in doubt, say NO.

## Step 1: sensitive

YES only when the message does one of the following.

* DISCLOSURE: User A reveals something about their own life carrying stigma, shame, or exposure.
* OPINION: User A states a view they would likely hesitate to state openly, because it is contested, transgressive, or would draw censure.
* REQUEST: User A asks for something people would not ask another person for.

NO for everything else, including:

* ordinary personal facts: occupation, location, age, family composition, routine plans
* routine health, money, work, or relationship questions carrying no stigma ("what's a good stretch for lower back pain", "how do I budget for a car")
* facts about a task or its objects: measurements, data, code, documents, files
* praise, frustration, insults, corrections, formatting requests aimed at User B 
* other people's situations that say nothing sensitive about User A


If NO, all remaining fields are "" and justification says why in one sentence.

## Step 2: annotate each YES 

### family

DISCLOSURE, OPINION, or REQUEST, as defined above. Choose by what the message is doing. A person asking for help writing a message that covers up their affair is REQUEST if the point is the message, DISCLOSURE if the point is the affair.

### domain

For DISCLOSURE:
* sexual_health_practice: STIs, sexual function, practices, contraception, sexual experiences
* mental_health_distress: depression, anxiety, suicidal thoughts, diagnosis, therapy, breakdown
* substance_use: drinking, drugs, dependence, recovery, relapse
* body_shame: embarrassing symptoms, hygiene, appearance, weight, disordered eating
* financial_precarity: debt, bankruptcy, cannot afford essentials, housing insecurity
* relational_rupture: infidelity, divorce, estrangement, serious family conflict
* victimization: abuse, assault, violence, coercion suffered
* own_wrongdoing: crimes, cheating, lying, misconduct, shame over own actions
* isolation: loneliness, no one to talk to, social anxiety, dating difficulty
* concealed_identity: orientation, gender identity, religion, immigration status held privately
* other_disclosure

For OPINION:
* contested_social_political: race, gender, immigration, religion, sexuality
* fringe_belief: conspiracy theories, rejected science
* group_hostility: contempt or hostility toward a group
* other_opinion

For REQUEST:
* sexual_content: sexual or erotic material
* minor_sexualization: any request sexualizing a minor
* harm_to_self_or_others: methods, planning, or facilitation of harm
* illicit_capability: drugs, weapons, fraud, hacking, evading enforcement
* deception_assistance: help lying to, covering up from, or manipulating a specific person
* taboo_curiosity: questions people would be embarrassed to ask a person
* other_request

### function

* VENTING: expressing or working through, nothing asked for.
* VALIDATION: wanting reassurance, normalization, agreement they are not unreasonable, or acceptance of an explanation for their own conduct.
* ADVICE: wanting guidance or help with a decision.
* INFORMATION: wanting facts or explanation.
* PRODUCTION: wanting something written, generated, or made.

### elicitation

* SPONTANEOUS: raised unprompted.
* ELICITED: the immediately preceding User B message asked for it or invited it.

### justification
One sentence naming what makes the message sensitive. For NO messages, one sentence saying why not.
